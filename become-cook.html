Host Kitchen Requirements

Ensure you satisfy these essential categories before submitting your registration details.

🏠

Kitchen & Storage

  • A sanitary, ventilated cooking area
  • Continuous running water and clean workstation surfaces
  • Cold refrigeration units to preserve raw ingredients
  • Cozy seating area for eaters (dine-in hosts only)
📋

Identity & Certifications

  • Valid Government ID (Ghana Card or Passport)
  • Active phone connection linked to Mobile Money
  • Ghana FDA recommended Food Handler medical check
  • At least 2 clean kitchen/food preparation photographs
👩‍🍳

Skill & Culinary Standards

  • Proven ability to prepare delicious traditional recipes
  • Understanding of local spice tolerances & heat levels
  • Clear disclosure of groundnuts, seafood, or common allergens
  • Consistent cooking and preparation times
📱

Technology & Commitments

  • Active iOS or Android smartphone with YenDidii App
  • Fast booking response time (under 30 minutes)
  • Flexibility to list session slots once or twice a week
  • Open, welcoming attitude to neighbors in your area

Academy Curriculum Overview

The interactive training course covers 5 foundational modules that every host cook should master.

1

Food Safety & Ghana FDA Standards

Familiarise yourself with the Public Health Act 2012 (Act 851), obtaining health handler cards, and sourcing clean pipeline/sachet water.

2

Kitchen Sanitation Rules

Strict handwashing rules (20 seconds), hairnets, clean chef aprons, and deep-sanitisation guidelines for knives/utensils.

3

Danger Zones & Cold Chain

Protecting food from bacterial multiplication between 5°C and 60°C. Storing raw meats safely on lower refrigeration shelves.

4

Hospitality & Dine-In Warmth

Providing the quintessential Ghanaian welcome (Akwaaba), serving clean water, and managing allergic disclosures for groundnuts.

5

YenDidii App Operations

Setting up listing rates, declaring portions, using "Join the Pot" events, and receiving direct Mobile Money wallet payouts.

Ghana Food Handlers Regulatory Compliance

Under local metropolitan and municipal regulations, individuals serving meals to the public require standard medical certificates. YenDidii strongly encourages all chefs to register with their district health inspectors. For direct assistance or inquiries on obtaining certification, read our academy modules or email support@yendidii.com.
