When you reserve a seat you are booking a place at a gathering run by an independent host. The price shown is per seat. Bookings are confirmed by the host. Please arrive on time, respect the host's home and house rules, and tell the host about allergies or dietary needs before the gathering. We are not responsible for allergic reactions or dietary issues the host was not told about.

Hosts are independent and are not employees or agents of YenDidii. Hosts may only be approved after a review, and we may remove a host or event that breaks these Terms.

5. Payments, cancellations and payouts

Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store full card details. Host payouts are made after an event is completed, less any service fee shown in the app. All seat bookings are final and non-refundable: hosts shop for and prepare fresh ingredients for your seat, so if you cancel or do not attend, you are not refunded. If a host cancels or an event does not go ahead, guests will be refunded as shown in the app. Contact hello@yendidii.com if something goes wrong with a payment.
