Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch to Kitchen Mode?

Go to your Profile tab in the bottom right, and tap "Switch to Kitchen Mode". Once in Kitchen Mode, you can add dishes to your menu and open your kitchen to receive orders from guests nearby.

How do I receive payments?

We use Paystack to process transactions. Once you list a dish and open a session, guests can pay directly in the app. Funds are credited directly to your registered bank account or mobile money wallet.
