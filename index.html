For Food Lovers & Guests

Craving something authentic? Skip the fast food and get access to fresh, home-cooked local specialties prepared with love by hosts in your community.

Guest Experience

A welcoming home dining experience.

Eat fresh homemade meals right at the host's place. Connect with local families, enjoy welcoming Ghanaian hospitality, and experience authentic communal dining in your neighborhood.

  • Dine-In or Pick Up: Choose to eat at the host's table for a warm, welcoming home experience, or grab your food to go.
  • Traditional Hospitality: Savor authentic local dishes like Jollof, Fufu & Light Soup, and Banku & Tilapia, cooked with passion in real Ghanaian home kitchens.
  • Communal Connections: Meet the talented neighbors preparing your food and support your community directly.

For Hosts

Turn your passion for cooking into a rewarding business. Share your delicious creations and monetize your culinary skills without the overhead of a restaurant.

Host Experience

Your kitchen, your rules.

Switch your account to Kitchen Mode instantly. List new dishes, manage your active menu, set available portions, and track your revenue in real time.

  • Kitchen Dashboard: Monitor your active sessions, orders, and total revenue.
  • Flexible Scheduling: Start cooking sessions whenever you're ready and toggle your status to open or closed.
  • Instant Swaps: One account is all you need to swap seamlessly between guest and host modes.

Geospatial Discovery

Find hosts in your immediate vicinity.

With our advanced Map View, you can locate hosts in your local area, check active menu prices instantly through price pills, and view location distances.

  • Real-time Location: Discover who is active in Adenta and surrounding neighborhoods.
  • Visual Map Pins: See real food photos directly on the map marker to know exactly what is cooking nearby.
