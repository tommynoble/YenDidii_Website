How it works

Three simple steps from hungry to seated.

1

Find a table

Browse gatherings near you on the home screen or the map, with real photos, dates and prices.

2

Reserve your seat

Choose your seats, tell the host about any allergies, and pay securely with mobile money or card.

3

Dine at your host's table

Arrive at the time shown and enjoy a home-cooked meal with your host and fellow guests.

For Guests

Skip the restaurant queue. Share a table with a Ghanaian host, taste home cooking you won't find on a menu, and meet people from your community.

A welcoming home dining experience.

Eat fresh homemade meals right at the host's place. Meet local families, enjoy welcoming Ghanaian hospitality, and experience communal dining in your neighborhood.

  • Dine-in at the host's home: You eat at the host's table for a warm, welcoming home experience. Every gathering shows its date, time and how many seats are left.
  • Traditional Hospitality: Savor authentic local dishes like Jollof, Fufu & Light Soup, and Banku & Tilapia, cooked with passion in real Ghanaian home kitchens.
  • Communal Connections: Meet the talented neighbors preparing your food and support your community directly.

For Hosts

Turn your love of cooking into income. Invite guests to your table, share your signature dishes, and earn without the overhead of a restaurant.

Host Experience

Your kitchen, your rules.

Switch your account to Kitchen Mode instantly. List new dishes, manage your active menu, set available portions, and track your revenue in real time.

  • Kitchen Dashboard: Monitor your active sessions, orders, and total revenue.
  • Flexible Scheduling: Start cooking sessions whenever you're ready and toggle your status to open or closed.
  • Instant Swaps: One account is all you need to swap seamlessly between guest and host modes.
